> ## 🛑 OUTREACH FREEZE — operator directive, 2026-09-02
>
> **No outreach of any kind until the operator lifts this.** No emails, no
> DMs, no forms, no conversations, no "just introducing ourselves". This
> applies to `backlink-pr`, `affiliate-pr` and `ad-sales` alike, and it
> overrides any pipeline step that reads as though it authorises contact.
>
> **Reason, operator's own words:** quality links come from messaging,
> emailing and talking to people — real relationships, not directory
> drops — and **the site needs to be more fleshed out before any PR
> campaign.** Pitching a thin site burns the contact permanently: you get
> one first impression per person, and spending it early costs more than
> waiting.
>
> **What the PR skills may still do, and should:** research targets,
> identify contact channels, draft pitches, record angles, flag what must
> never be claimed, and keep these logs current. Preparation is wanted.
> **Only the send is frozen.**
>
> Every entry logged during the freeze stays at `prospect`. A state beyond
> `prospect` during the freeze is a bug.
>
> **Exit condition — PROPOSED by `near-lead-product`, not yet decided.**
> The freeze needs a testable end or it becomes permanent by drift.
> Suggested gate, for the operator to accept, change or replace:
> substantially deeper coverage than today's ~60 places; the six locales
> genuinely complete rather than fallback-filled; at least one column with
> a real run of issues behind it; Search Console showing the site indexed
> rather than merely submitted; and zero broken hero images. Until the
> operator rules, treat the freeze as open-ended.

**Read this before any outreach.** The point of the log is that a future
session does not re-approach someone mid-conversation, or cheerfully
re-apply somewhere Near declined on editorial grounds.

Record for each: name, category/city, what was offered, contact channel,
the date of **every** touch, current state, terms, and which pieces (if
any) it touches. States: `prospect` / `pitched` / `negotiating` / `won` / `lost` /
`refused-by-us` — the last always with the reason, so the decision is not
silently reversed later.

**No email is ever sent without showing the operator the exact draft and
getting explicit approval.**
