# Near — Content Opportunities

What to write next, and why. Maintained by `near-seo` (Mode 1,
opportunity finding). Distinct from `content/requests.md`, which is
narrower: that file is demand generated by Near's own writing when a
piece wanted an internal link and found nothing. This file is demand from
outside — coverage gaps, unused capacity, and what people in each market
actually search for.

## On keyword tools

Near has no Google Keyword Planner or Search Console connection wired up
(see `BACKLOG.md`, SEO data gap). An attempt to drive Keyword Planner
through browser automation on 2026-08-31 failed outright — the Google Ads
SPA blocks script injection, timing out at 45s on text extraction.

That matters less than it sounds, for two reasons.

**Search Console has nothing to say yet.** near.tips went live on
2026-08-27. A site four days old with ~20 pages has essentially no
impression history. Connect it now — it only accumulates going forward,
so every week of delay is history you never get back — but don't wait on
it to choose what to write.

**Keyword volume answers a question Near isn't asking yet.** Volume data
arbitrates between phrasings when you're competing at scale. At 20 pages
the binding constraint is coverage: whole categories are empty, a Tier 1
hub has two places, and three of eight bylines have never written
anything. Those gaps are visible without a single search-volume figure,
and closing them is worth more than optimising a phrase.

Revisit this section once there are ~100 pages and three months of
Console data.

entering opposite seasons in September.** Brazil (São Paulo, Baixada
Santista) enters spring — beach season is *starting*, not peaking, which
is the correct time to publish (ahead of the crowd, not describing a
season already gone). London and SF enter autumn — the indoor/cultural
season (galleries, theatre, live music) ramps up as beach/park content
loses relevance there.
