Trunks has been on Santa Monica Boulevard for 35 years, and it reads like it: pool tables, sports on the TVs, a crowd that skews older and regular rather than dressed for a door. There's no cover charge and no dress code, which on this particular strip counts as a genuine point of difference — most of WeHo's nightlife is built around exactly those two filters.

## No changes, on purpose

Longtime owner Bill Holland ran the bar until his death in Palm Springs; brothers Waldo Duarte and Hector Omar Duarte took over in 2021 and were explicit about their plan, as [WEHOonline reported](https://wehoonline.com/new-owners-wehos-trunks-plan-changes/): keep it exactly as it was. That's unusual as a business decision and it shows in the room — a 2026 WEHOonline readers' poll still ranks Trunks among the city's best dive bars, the same category it's always run in rather than chasing a rebrand.

## What recent visitors actually say

Google's 469 reviews put it at 4.4 stars, and recent ones are specific. One visitor, three months before this writing, called a night there "electric." Reviews aren't uniform: one regular, a month ago, described an $89 tab for two margaritas and three shots with no warning about the pour; another flagged a rude bartender over water. Worth knowing, not a dealbreaker on a bar whose appeal is unfussiness. A mile east, [Bar Lubitsch](https://near.tips/place/bar-lubitsch-weho) goes the opposite way: Russian theming, weekly comedy.
