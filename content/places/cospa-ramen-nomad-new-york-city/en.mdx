Cospa Ramen serves ultra-thin noodles in a lighter pork broth at 36 West 26th Street, Manhattan. [HAND Hospitality](https://www.handhospitality.com/handhistory) takes its cue from Nagahama's worker stalls; [Yomitime](https://yomitime.com/cospa-ramen-nyc-open/) places that tradition around Fukuoka's fish market. Counter seats make the small-menu format particularly useful for a solo meal.

Start with the original ramen, topped with chashu and scallions. Save some broth for **kaedama**, a separate helping of noodles added when the first portion is nearly finished. Spicy ramen and karaage round out the short menu. Both ramen bases contain pork; vegetarians need another option.

Cospa Ramen is inexpensive by Manhattan restaurant standards: its [ordering menu](https://order.online/store/towa-new-york-28072523), checked September 8, 2026, lists original ramen at US$9.99 per bowl, spicy at US$10.99, extra noodles at US$2 and an egg at US$1.50, before tax and tip. The ordering page retains the former Towa name and currently disables online orders; these are listed prices, not a delivery offer.

Walk in and order by QR code. The [August 28 announcement](https://www.instagram.com/cosparamen/p/Dclwvl0xxzv/) gives 10:30am–2am hours, but [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/new-york/reviews/cospa-ramen) reports earlier sellouts. [A Google reviewer](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cospa+Ramen+36+W+26th+St+New+York) also reports a long wait. Allow time before closing.
