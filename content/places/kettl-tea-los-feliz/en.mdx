Kettl is the only US-based Japanese tea company fully incorporated in Japan, with an actual office in Fukuoka — not a branding detail, since it's what lets the shop carry specific, named cultivars rather than a generic "matcha" menu. Founders Zach and Minami Mangan opened their Brooklyn location first; this Los Feliz cafe, running since Spring 2024, is the company's flagship on the West Coast.

## A tasting counter, not just a counter

The four-seat tasting bar runs guided sessions like "Teas of Japan," where staff walk small groups through the differences between cultivars rather than just pulling a shot. One visitor, two weeks before this writing, described a Sunday-morning session as busy but unhurried, with staff "spending time with us and guiding us through the tasting."

![Kettl Los Feliz, a Japanese tea cafe in Los Angeles](https://near.tips/places/kettl-tea-los-feliz/hero.jpg)

Google's 200 reviews put it at 4.4 stars, and the specifics in them are the tell: reviewers name actual cultivars — Hukuju, Suiteki from Nishio — rather than just "the matcha latte." One reviewer, four months before this writing, specifically praised that the matcha wasn't pre-sweetened, calling the result "nutty, velvety" rather than generic-sweet.
