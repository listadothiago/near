The Hummingbird is chef-owner Ricardo Zarate's current restaurant, and his background is the actual reason to go: Peruvian-born, with stints at Picca, Rosaline and Causita that earned him Michelin and James Beard recognition before this smaller Echo Park room. The cooking here is Nikkei — Peruvian technique crossed with Japanese ingredients and plating — rather than fusion as a marketing word.

## Not every review agrees, and that's worth knowing

One Peruvian reviewer, a month before this writing, was openly skeptical going in — questioning whether fusion cooking dilutes an already-strong cuisine rather than improving it. It's a real culinary opinion worth weighing, not a knock on the food's quality. Other recent reviewers land differently: one, three months before this writing, called it a place that "quietly gets everything right," with a focused menu and fresh ingredients.

![The Hummingbird, a Peruvian-Japanese restaurant in Echo Park](https://near.tips/places/the-hummingbird-echo-park/hero.jpeg)

Google's 197 reviews put it at 4.6 stars. The most-ordered dishes are the Opal Ceviche and a passion fruit panna cotta for dessert — a detail that signals the kitchen's range beyond raw fish. One reviewer flagged the prices as steep for the portions; at $40-90 a person, it's priced like the chef's resume, not like a neighborhood spot.
