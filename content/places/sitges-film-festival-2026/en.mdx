Brian De Palma's *Carrie* came out in 1976. Sitges is marking its fiftieth by putting it at the centre of the 59th edition of the festival, which runs from **8 to 18 October 2026** — eleven days, in a seaside town of narrow streets that spends the rest of the year not looking like a horror festival at all.

That is the pitch, and it is worth taking literally: the whole town gets taken over, not one arts centre in it.

## What is actually programmed

The festival's own announcement for the 2026 edition sets out four strands beyond the competition: a retrospective of fantastic classics, an exploration of Asian genre filmmaking, a tribute to Spanish cinema's contribution to the genre, and the *Carrie* anniversary. Alongside them, the SGAE Foundation and the festival are running [the second edition of the Josefina Molina Award](https://sitgesfilmfestival.com/en/noticies/cutting-edge-fantastic-genre-cinema-lands-sitges-26) for the best fantastic feature screenplay by a woman, a single prize of €10,000.

The dates are confirmed by [the town's own tourism board](https://www.sitgesanytime.com/en/calendar/pln330/sitges-international-fantastic-film-festival-of-catalonia), which also carries the two things that make the festival visibly different from a normal one: the Zombie Walk, and the King Kong Area of stalls and stands that colonises the seafront.

## Where the screens are, and one you should not count on

The main room is the 1,380-seat Auditori inside the Meliá Sitges hotel, out at Aiguadolç — a fifteen-minute walk east of the old town, which surprises people who booked in the centre expecting everything to be within three streets. Screenings also run at <NearLink slug="casino-prado-sitges">Cinema Prado</NearLink>, which is where the [first fantastic-cinema week was held in 1968](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sitges_Film_Festival) and is the closest thing the festival has to a birthplace.

The one to check rather than assume is El Retiro, the other historic society hall, which has appeared on festival venue lists for decades. [Its own site records that renovation work began on 4 May 2024](https://elretirositges.cat/remodelacio/) — the hall stripped back to Miquel Utrillo's 1916 vaults after a false ceiling from 1970 came out — and publishes no reopening date. Don't build a schedule around it without confirming.

Don't miss the walk between venues at night. Sitges is small enough that the gap between an Auditori screening and a Prado one is a walk along the seafront rather than a transfer, and the Passeig de la Ribera during festival week, banner-lit and full of people arguing about a film, is most of the reason to come here rather than stream the same titles in six months.

October in Sitges is also still Sitges: <NearLink slug="parrots-pub-sitges">Parrots</NearLink> and the Joan Tarrida strip are ten minutes from the Prado and do not go quiet because there is a festival on.
