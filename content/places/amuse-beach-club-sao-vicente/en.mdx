**AMUSE Beach Club**, on top of Ilha Porchat, is the coast's answer to a real gap: Baixada Santista's LGBTQIA+ scene doesn't have many large-format venues, and most of what genuinely holds space for it is smaller and more local, like <NearLink slug="quiosque-da-cris-sao-vicente">Quiosque da Cris</NearLink> down on Praia do Itararé. AMUSE is the bigger, later, louder version of that same need.

## A room built around the view

Ilha Porchat is already one of the Baixada's best-known viewpoints — a rocky outcrop at the edge of São Vicente with a genuine panorama over the U-shaped Bay of Santos. AMUSE is built on top of it, and by [QLIST's venue listing](https://qlist.app/venues/Saint-Vincent/AMUSE-Beach-Club/cVZvdjdLaUZSMTgrakpjcHNyYjI5dw/pt) and its own promotional material, the sea view isn't incidental to the pitch — it's the whole point of building a party venue up here rather than down at street level. The space runs both indoor and outdoor dance floors, letting a night move between the two depending on the crowd and the weather, with music running from pop through house and electronic sets.

## What actually happens there

This isn't a one-off pop-up — the venue's own [Instagram (@amuseclub)](https://www.instagram.com/amuseclub/) shows a full, live calendar running well into September 2026, including a fifth-anniversary "HUMAN" party and a steady run of themed weekend nights branded as "Baile da AMUSE." That consistency matters more than any single event: a venue that's been running weekly programming for five years is a fixture, not a trend piece.

Some of that programming crosses over with **House of Mamba Negra**, a ballroom/voguing collective that's shown up on AMUSE's own calendar alongside other local promoters. Near couldn't independently verify House of Mamba Negra's own home base or standing schedule this pass — worth flagging honestly rather than writing around it — so this page describes the AMUSE/Mamba Negra crossover as what it verifiably is: a real, current booking relationship, not a claim about a separate standalone scene. [Vamos Gay's regional coast guide](https://vamosgay.com/latin-america/brazil/sao-paulo/gay-sao-vicente-santos-guaruja-praia-grande-gls-litoral/) lists AMUSE among the coast's handful of genuinely LGBTQIA+-anchored nightlife spots, distinct from the mixed-crowd beach bars that dominate the rest of the Baixada's options.

## Who it's actually for

AMUSE reads as mainstream-gay rather than deliberately alternative — a big room built to run a good party more than to hold a subculture space — but "mainstream" here doesn't mean generic. The booking mix that includes a ballroom-adjacent collective on select nights suggests a venue willing to reach past its default crowd, which is worth more in a region with this few large LGBTQIA+ venues than a purist read of the word "mainstream" might suggest.

## Practicalities

AMUSE is at Alameda Paulo Gonçalves, 680, on Ilha Porchat, São Vicente — a short taxi or rideshare from central Santos or São Vicente, since the hilltop location isn't an easy walk from the beachfront below. Programming is weekend-weighted; check the venue's own Instagram or WhatsApp line before a visit, since specific night themes and door policy change week to week. Tickets and VIP lists are generally sold in advance through the venue's own channels rather than at the door.
