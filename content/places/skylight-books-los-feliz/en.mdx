Skylight Books has been on N. Vermont Avenue since November 1, 1996, a few blocks from the Sunset/Vermont Metro station and right next to the Los Feliz 3 cinema. It grew enough by 2026 that it opened a second location in Eagle Rock — while keeping this store as the flagship, not folding it into a rebrand.

## The Annex is the actual draw

Next door, the Arts Annex runs a real zine and art-book collection rather than a token display case — reviewers consistently call it out by name over the main store. One visitor, two months before this writing, singled out "so many amazing art books" and the zine selection specifically, on top of staff recommendations they called reliably good.

![Skylight Books, an independent bookstore in Los Feliz](https://near.tips/places/skylight-books-los-feliz/hero.jpeg)

Google's 954 reviews put it at 4.7 stars. Multiple recent reviewers describe it as small but tightly curated rather than exhaustive — a point in its favor for browsing, a limitation if you're after a specific obscure title. A monthly book club, Coyotes Book Club, meets on the fourth Tuesday; check current listings before planning around it.
