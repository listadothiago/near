Dubelato São Carlos sits in Jardim Paraíso, directly across from Igreja Nossa Senhora de Fátima and a short walk from [Caverna da Gordura](/place/caverna-da-gordura-sao-carlos) on the same street. It's a branch of a 23-store gelato chain that started in Poços de Caldas, Minas Gerais, in 2017 — no local origin story to tell here, just a straightforward, well-run scoop shop.

## Consistent, not flashy

Reviews describe exactly that: one visitor's whole review is "Gelato bom, atendimento excelente e lugar agradável" — good gelato, excellent service, pleasant place — and that's a fair summary of the pattern across the sample. It's not unanimous: one reviewer flagged understaffing on a Friday afternoon that led to a real line, and another said flatly that competitors offer tastier product for the same money. Worth knowing both, rather than only the average.

![Gelato tubs in pistachio, berry, passion fruit and chocolate, with wafer cookies, from Dubelato's own Instagram, São Carlos](https://near.tips/places/dubelato-sao-carlos/hero.jpg)

Open Monday through Saturday from noon to 10pm and Sunday 11am to 9pm — late enough for an after-dinner stop most nights of the week. Delivery and full flavor list through the [official Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/dubelatosaocarlos/) and iFood.
