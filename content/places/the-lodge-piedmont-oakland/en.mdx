Piedmont Avenue is one of Oakland's most walkable commercial strips — restaurants, cafes, a movie theater, independent shops — and at 3758 sits The Lodge, a neighborhood bar that briefly went dark in January 2026 before new owners Chris Cashin and Kris Gordon reopened it that April.

## A reopening the neighborhood actually noticed

The turnaround got real local attention. The [SF Chronicle](https://www.sfchronicle.com/eastbay/) covered it twice, and [Oaklandside](https://oaklandside.org/), [KRON4](https://www.kron4.com/) and other Bay Area outlets ran their own pieces on the new ownership — a lot of press for a single neighborhood bar's change of hands, and a sign of how tightly Piedmont Avenue holds onto its regular spots.

The bar kept its name and its room rather than rebranding from scratch, which reads as a deliberate choice: a strip like this rewards continuity over reinvention. It sits within easy walking distance of the rest of the corridor's restaurants and cafes, and a few blocks from the historic Piedmont Theatre, making it an easy stop before or after dinner elsewhere on the avenue.

For the rest of a Piedmont Avenue evening, the strip's restaurants and the nearby Piedmont Theatre are both worth folding into the same walk.
