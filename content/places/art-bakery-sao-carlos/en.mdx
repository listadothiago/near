Art Bakery runs out of Rua Dr. Orlando Damiano in Jardim Macarengo as a natural-fermentation bakery and café under Fernanda Seridorio and Ravi Hummel — the kind of couple-run operation that shows up in the reviews as much as the bread does, since the owners reply individually to nearly every one, thanks included. A [feature on São Carlos em Rede](https://saocarlosemrede.com.br/art-bakery/) covers the pitch directly: the bakery is built around rescuing what a long, slow fermentation actually does for bread, rather than treating "artisanal" as a label to slap on a fast dough.

## The bread does the theatrics, not just the counter

The lineup covers Ma Petite-style blended-flour loaves, Italian, baguette, brioche, seven-grain and olive ciabatta, alongside croissants (around R$28) and a 50% whole-wheat loaf (around R$30.50) per the [official iFood menu](https://www.ifood.com.br/delivery/sao-carlos-sp/art-bakery-padaria-e-cafeteria-jardim-macarengo/89796886-de37-486f-9f63-09226471f3c8). One Google reviewer, after visiting multiple long-fermentation bakeries, called this one the best of the lot — croissants included. Presentation gets called out specifically in reviews too, not just taste, which tracks with a business whose own Instagram bio reads "arte em forma de pão," art in the form of bread.

![A basket of fresh-baked bread held by an Art Bakery staff member, São Carlos](https://near.tips/places/art-bakery-sao-carlos/hero.jpg)

Budget-wise, Google's own diner-reported range is R$40-60 per person, based on 68 people who logged what they spent — on the higher end for a São Carlos bakery, and reviewers say so directly, with one noting the ciabatta specifically justifies the price even if the overall menu runs a bit steep.

## Not every plate lands exactly like the photo

One recent, specific complaint is worth passing on straight: reviewer Carolina Y. ordered a cinnamon roll that arrived looking nothing like its iFood listing photo — an oversized, cream-heavy version she found too messy to eat by hand. The bakery's own reply agreed outright: the delivery-app photo was outdated and didn't represent the current product, a rare case of a business confirming a complaint rather than deflecting it.

![A slice of chocolate-spread pão de mel held up at Art Bakery, São Carlos](https://near.tips/places/art-bakery-sao-carlos/pao-de-mel.jpg)

Google flags the business as LGBTQ+-friendly. The [official Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/artbakery.sc/) runs as a product-and-atmosphere feed rather than an events calendar, with no dated programming found in current posts. It's closed Mondays, open Tuesday through Friday 8am-7pm and Saturday 8am-2pm (the café counter closes half an hour before the bakery does).
