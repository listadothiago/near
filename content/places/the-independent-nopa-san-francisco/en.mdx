Divisadero Street runs through NOPA as a strip of bars, coffee and record shops, and at 628 Divisadero sits a converted theater space that's spent years as one of San Francisco's most dependable mid-size rooms: [The Independent](https://www.theindependentsf.com/), capacity right around 500.

## A room bands actually want to play

That number matters more than it sounds. Too small and a touring act with real draw skips the city; too big and a band on the way up plays to a half-empty floor that kills the room's energy. The Independent sits in the sweet spot — [Live Nation's venue calendar](https://www.livenation.com/venue/KovZpZAE6e7A/the-independent-events) runs indie rock next to hip-hop next to electronic and stand-up comedy, often the same week, which is a genuinely broad booking range for a room this size.

The floor is general-admission standing, with a small elevated platform toward the back and a balcony for people who'd rather watch from above the crowd than inside it — useful on a sold-out night when the main floor gets tight. [Songkick's venue page](https://www.songkick.com/venues/13488-independent) and [SF Station's listing](https://www.sfstation.com/the-independent-e989706) both track it as a steady part of the city's touring circuit, the kind of room a band remembers as the good stop between a dive bar and an arena.

For another night out on Divisadero before or after a show, the strip's bars and coffee spots are a five-minute walk in either direction.
