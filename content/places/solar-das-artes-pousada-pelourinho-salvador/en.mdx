Solar das Artes sits on R. das Laranjeiras in Pelourinho, Salvador's cobblestoned historic center, a five-minute walk from Praça das Artes. It's a small, independently run inn inside a colonial mansion — not one of the bigger, more marketed hotels in the same few blocks, and closer in spirit to staying somewhere a local family actually runs.

## An inn that's also a gallery

The concept is literal, not decorative: local artists exhibit and sell work throughout the property, so the walls change over time rather than staying fixed as generic hotel art. Reviewers name a host, Cris, specifically and repeatedly as warm and hands-on — the kind of detail that shows up when a small operation is genuinely run by the people who own it, not a rotating desk staff. Rooms run comfortable and well air-conditioned; some top-floor rooms come with a large private terrace, worth asking for at booking.

## Before you book

The street is flagged in reviews as safe and well-lit, useful to know in a historic center where that varies block to block. The pousada's own website was unreachable at the time of writing — book through a platform listing or call ahead at (71) 3481-0085 rather than relying on a dead link. It sits within walking distance of the Catedral Basílica and Salvador's main Pelourinho churches and museums, so plan on covering the neighborhood on foot.
