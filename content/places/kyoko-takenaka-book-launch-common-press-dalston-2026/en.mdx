Kyoko Takenaka — an artist and photographer whose work moves between the two disciplines rather than staying in one — brings the London leg of her book launch to [The Common Press Dalston](/en/place/the-common-press-dalston-london) on September 23, 2026. The venue's own listing describes it as "an evening of conversation, photography, performance and exploring Kyoko's experience," which is a broader billing than most book launches carry — this isn't a reading with a signing table after, it's closer to a small performance built around the book.

Tobi Adebajo joins as the evening's featured guest, though [the venue's event page](https://www.commonpress.co.uk/event-details/kyoko-takenaka-im-here-i-love-you-book-launch-ft-tobi-adebajo) doesn't spell out their specific role beyond that credit — worth asking at the door or checking the venue's Instagram closer to the date if that detail matters to you.

Doors run 6:30–9pm on a Wednesday, which puts it comfortably in after-work-drink territory for anyone in Dalston or Stoke Newington already. Booking runs through [Outsavvy](https://www.outsavvy.com/event/39670/kyoko-takenaka-im-here-i-love-you-book-launch-ft-tobi-adebajo); no ticket price is listed on the venue's own page at the time of writing, so don't assume free entry — confirm on the booking link before making it your whole evening's plan.
