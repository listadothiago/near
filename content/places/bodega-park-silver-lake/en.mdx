Bodega Park sits where Black Hogg, a chef-driven gastropub, used to run — same ownership, new concept, built around the New York bodega sandwich rather than small plates. The shift shows in the name on their own Instagram handle, still @blackhoggsandwiches, a small detail that reads like an inside joke about the pivot rather than an oversight.

## A real bodega sandwich, with a Korean accent

The chopped cheese is the anchor, but the menu runs it alongside a pork bulgogi sandwich and Peruvian-style chicken breakfast burritos — the owners' Korean and LA backgrounds layered onto a New York format rather than a straight import. Food & Wine ranked it the second-best sandwich shop in the country, a real trade-press call rather than a listicle blurb.

![Bodega Park, a sandwich shop in Silver Lake](https://near.tips/places/bodega-park-silver-lake/hero.jpeg)

Google's 251 reviews put it at 4.6 stars, and recent visitors name actual sandwiches rather than giving a vague thumbs-up. One reviewer, a month before this writing, singled out the chopped cheese and "basic" turkey sandwich both; another, six months before, called the bacon-egg-and-cheese and bulgogi pork "amazing" on the same visit.
