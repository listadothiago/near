Hell's Kitchen lost a bar and got a bigger one back. [Monkey Thief](https://www.monkeythiefbar.com/about/) opened on 9th Avenue in late 2024 as a Pan-Asian cocktail room built around its founders' time living and traveling through Asia — then shut down abruptly in 2026 after a licensing dispute, the kind of sudden closure [w42st](https://w42st.com/post/monkey-thief-finds-a-new-home-on-9th-ave/), the neighborhood's own indie outlet, was first to report in real time rather than after the fact. The team didn't relocate across town; they took over the building next door and rebuilt.

What reopened on 14 August 2026 at 754 9th Avenue is a bigger operation split into three connected spaces, per [Cititour's](https://cititour.com/NYC_News/Monkey-Thief-Is-Back-in-Hells-Kitchen-With-a-Bigger-Reimagined-Home/10938) walkthrough of the new build: The Court, styled after a Southeast Asian street market as the entry room; The Train Car, running the length of the space with a classic-rail-travel bar down its middle; and The Den at the back, the quieter room for the end of the night. It's a real expansion, not a like-for-like replacement of what the licensing issue took away.

The drink list keeps the original bar's signature moves — [Resy's](https://blog.resy.com/2024/10/monkey-thief-nyc/) coverage of the first opening singled out the 7 Long Years, a spicy margarita run through green chiles and sesame, and the Major Tom, a cocktail built on Thai tom kha soup flavors rather than the usual citrus-and-sugar template. The food side runs oysters at market price, an $18 beef koi, an $18 white fish crudo and $8 fries, per the [official menu](https://www.monkeythiefbar.com/menu/food/) — sourced this session, though the full cocktail-list pricing wasn't posted publicly at time of writing, so budget toward a standard Manhattan cocktail-bar range rather than a confirmed number until that's verified.

Recent [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/monkey-thief-new-york) reviews (4.9 from 68 ratings as of this write-up) call the cocktails the strongest in the neighborhood and the staff attentive without being precious about it — read as an early verdict on the new build specifically, since the reopening is barely a month old.
