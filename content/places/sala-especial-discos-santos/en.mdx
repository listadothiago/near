Sala Especial Discos runs a listening bar on Rua Frei Gaspar in Centro, Santos, calling itself the Baixada Santista's first — arched, backlit shelves of vinyl double as the room's own architecture, with a DJ booth built into the same wall. It's the kind of room where the record collection isn't decoration for a bar; the bar is what got built around the records. Gezellig is the word for it: the Dutch term for a warmth that's cozy and convivial at once, and it's a better fit here than "cool" or "hip."

## VORTEX, and the rest of the calendar

The room's flagship night is [VORTEX](https://www.sympla.com.br/evento/vortex-na-sala-especial-discos/3582854), a self-described "festa de rock para dançar" — a rock party built for dancing. The next date is confirmed: Saturday, October 3, 2026, from 10pm, tickets capped at 50 and sold in advance through Sympla. Beyond VORTEX, the venue's own Instagram runs a full monthly lineup — Club Caribe DJ nights, themed parties, and citywide vinyl fairs.

![The arched vinyl-record display shelving and DJ booth at Sala Especial Discos, Santos](https://near.tips/places/sala-especial-discos-santos/hero.jpg)

Reviews on Google are few but specific: one calls it "Ótima Vibe. Local único" — great vibe, a unique place — echoing exactly what a first-time visitor would clock walking in. Another flags genuinely good vegan snacks alongside the drinks list, a detail that shows up unprompted rather than as a marketed feature. A separate, plainer record shop under the same name operates at Rua XV de Novembro 146, for anyone after the buying-and-selling side without the bar.
