The building on the corner of Martin Luther King Jr Way and West Grand has a bar in it, a kitchen serving oxtail, and a dance floor. [Yelp has it listed as closed](https://www.yelp.com/biz/starline-social-club-oakland).

That listing is wrong, and it has been wrong for months.

## The part that is true: it did close

The original Starline Social Club opened in 2015 in an 1893 Odd Fellows hall — three storeys, a 400-capacity ballroom on the second floor, a ground-floor sideroom the owners [described as a speakeasy](https://starlinesocialclub.com/about/). The owners bought the building in 2018, then [put it on the market in October 2020](https://www.kqed.org/arts/13887215/end-of-an-era-oakland-venue-starline-social-club-is-on-the-market) to avoid a foreclosure as the pandemic ground on.

It really did shut. The last show was a New Year's Eve party headlined by Los Rakas on 31 December 2022.

"We have had an amazing run but it is time for new operators to take over the space and create something new for Oakland," Sam White of the club [announced at the time](https://www.kqed.org/arts/13922825/starline-social-club-to-close-at-the-end-of-the-year).

## New operators, three names, one address

They took him at his word. The room was briefly A Capella. New owners took over the following January and [opened as Bad Decision in late February 2026](https://oaklandside.org/2026/04/07/nudi-blue-cheese-board-sodo-quebrada-opening/). By that May they had reworked the concept, [brought in Sistas Wings to run the kitchen and put the old name back up](https://oaklandside.org/2026/05/06/starline-sable-lounge-lodge-sanaa-shake-shack-open/).

Sistas Wings is the reason to eat here rather than only drink: wings, yes, but also oxtail, pork chops and lamb chops.

The programming has moved. Where the old Starline booked rap shows, indie rock and weekly karaoke, the [current room](https://www.instagram.com/starlinecomplex/) runs DJ nights and dance collectives, with Urban Prodigy as resident promoter and recurring nights that carry their own names.

## Check the hours before you go

The hours have already changed once. The relaunch ran Wednesday to Saturday from 5pm; the venue's own Instagram bio now reads Wednesday to Saturday, 3pm to 2am or last call, with more days promised. This is a room still deciding what it is, so read the bio rather than any listing.

Nobody told the aggregators. Yelp carries the closed banner and the previous operation's hours. Reddit's most recent thread about the place is two years old. The East Bay food press caught it and almost nobody else did.
