SAN sits on R. Conselheiro Pedro Luiz in Rio Vermelho, Salvador's bohemian late-night neighborhood, a few blocks from the water. It's one of the city's established LGBTQIA+ nightclubs — a lounge space with a light-effects dance floor and DJs mixing electro through most of the night, not a pop-up or a seasonal thing.

## What the regulars actually say

The reviews split cleanly into two threads. On the music and space: "ambiente bonito espaçoso, bem organizado... música excelente, os DJs são incríveis" — a beautiful, spacious, well-organized room with excellent music and great DJs, from a [reviewer on Google](https://www.google.com/maps/place/SAN/@-13.0125452,-38.489931,17z/data=!4m6!3m5!1s0x71603679bb1a77d:0xc5e10238b5026a0d!8m2!3d-13.0125452!4d-38.489931), 2026. One longtime regular writes that SAN feels like a second home, having gone since he came of age. The recurring complaint runs the other way: a review flags the bathroom as "EXTREMAMENTE PRECÁRIA... quase sempre imunda" — extremely rundown, almost always dirty. Both show up often enough in the same listing to be worth knowing going in, not just the star average.

## On the calendar

[Festa Brasileiragem](https://www.salvadordabahia.com/eventos/festa-brasileiragem-com-melody/) lands at SAN on October 17, 2026, with singer Melody headlining from 10pm to 4am and an open bar covering Amstel, gin and national vodka plus mixers. For the wider agenda, SAN posts a monthly poster to its [own Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/sanboate.rv/) rather than running a public listings feed — check there before a specific night. Expect R$80-100 a person once cover and drinks are counted in.
