Bar Lubitsch sits on the Santa Monica Boulevard stretch known as WeHo's Russian-speaking district, and it leans into it: Art Deco detail, a century-old look despite opening in the 2000s, and three rooms that each feel like a different decade. Owner Sean McPherson, who runs several LA bars, opened it with manager Jared Meisler and named it for the filmmaker Ernst Lubitsch.

## A real comedy room, not a side gig

The back room runs [Totally!](https://www.netflixisajokefest.com/venues/bar-lubitsch), a weekly indie standup showcase that's booked comics with HBO and Netflix credits — a rarer thing than it sounds for a bar that isn't primarily a comedy club. It happens on Wednesdays on an ongoing basis; check the show's own listings for the exact date before planning around it.

![Bar Lubitsch, a Russian-themed vodka bar in West Hollywood](https://near.tips/places/bar-lubitsch-weho/hero.webp)

Google's 572 reviews put it at 4.3 stars, and more than one recent visitor calls out the room's acoustics specifically rather than just the drinks — one reviewer, six months before this writing, said the acoustics were "the bomb" independent of how much she'd had to drink. Another, the same month, mentioned catching a comedy night and being surprised by the photobooth. No food is served on-site; neighboring Jones Hollywood covers that. For a completely different register on the same boulevard, [Trunks](https://near.tips/place/trunks-weho), a mile west, skips the theming for pool tables and no cover charge.
