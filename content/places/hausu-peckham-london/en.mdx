Hausu puts a kitchen counter and [vintage hi-fi](https://www.hausulondon.co.uk/) inside Peckham Rye's former ticket office. Dinner comes with a view of the cooks at work; [Time Out's review](https://www.timeout.com/london/restaurants/hausu) also finds a walk-in bar. An [upstairs listening room](https://www.wallpaper.com/travel/bars/upstairs-at-hausu-listening-bar-peckham) extends the evening. The station address makes this an easy place to build an evening around.

Save room for the toasted-rice ice cream with five-spice caramel. Time Out praised it, and [The Picky Glutton's July 2026 review](https://pickyglutton.com/2026/07/06/hausu-review/) found it the standout dessert while judging the cooking uneven overall. That reviewer was less taken with the much-discussed prawn toast and preferred the quieter foyer seating. Ask about that room if conversation matters more than watching the kitchen.

Hausu's [September announcement](https://www.instagram.com/hausu_london/p/Dc50-Ksoqs6/), checked on 7 September 2026, says the kitchen will start at 5pm from the following week, with three early-set courses for £30 per person until 6:30pm. Its website still shows the older 6pm weekday opening. Check the booking time directly. The linked sample menu specifies a discretionary 12.5% service charge; confirm that applies to your booking too.
