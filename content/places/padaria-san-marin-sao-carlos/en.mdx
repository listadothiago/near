San Marin sits on Rua Dom Pedro II in Vila Monteiro (Gleba I), a hand-painted leaf mural and "San Marin" signage marking a covered terrace that runs as a bakery, café and lunch counter across the whole day rather than closing after the breakfast rush. It's open Monday through Saturday 7am to 7pm and Sunday 7am to 1pm — hours that cover breakfast, a proper prato feito lunch, and afternoon coffee without a gap.

## The sandwich counter, not just the pastry case

Google's own highlighted-menu data names the Bauru Clássico — the classic Brazilian pressed sandwich of roast beef, tomato and melted cheese — and Queijo Quente as the two items customers order most. That's a useful signal on its own: a bakery whose most-ordered items are hot sandwiches rather than sweets is built around lunch, not just coffee and croissants. One Local Guide's review calls San Marin outright "sem dúvidas, minha padaria favorita" — without a doubt my favorite bakery — praising consistently fresh product and a genuinely warm welcome.

![The covered dining terrace at San Marin, with its hand-painted mural and signage, São Carlos](https://near.tips/places/padaria-san-marin-sao-carlos/hero.jpg)

Budget-wise, Google's own diner-reported range is R$40-60 per person, based on 57 people who logged what they spent. Not every review agrees it's worth that: one visitor found the room genuinely loud — a bell rings constantly and staff talk over each other inside — and called the prices a little steep for what's on the plate, a fair complaint worth weighing alongside the praise rather than dropping.
