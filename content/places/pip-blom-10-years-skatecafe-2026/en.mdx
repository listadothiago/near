Pip Blom’s tenth anniversary brings an old bandmate back behind the kit. [The official programme](https://skatecafe.weticket.io/pip-blom-10-years) confirms Gini Cameron’s one-off return for the 24 September 2026 show at <NearLink slug="skatecafe-noord-amsterdam">Skatecafé in Amsterdam-Noord</NearLink>. The anniversary EP, PB10, reworks earlier songs; the concert promises older material that has been absent from recent sets.

The bill spreads across two rooms. My Purse and Fellatio join Pip Blom in Skatecafé, while Scram C Baby, Djalan Djalan and Baby Berserk play Dick & Dick. The published 20:00–01:00 window covers the evening, not a confirmed start time for Pip Blom’s set.

## Tickets and dinner

On 10 September 2026, [WeTicket](https://skatecafe.weticket.io/pip-blom-10-years) listed regular admission at €15 plus a €1 fee, and late-tier admission at €17.50 plus €1. The cheaper early tier was unavailable. Availability can change before checkout.

This concert is marked all ages. Dinner requires a separate reservation; the venue’s [restaurant](https://www.skatecafe.nl/restaurant/) serves from 18:00. Choose your meal timing around the programme, and keep the full evening free if you want to catch the other bands.
