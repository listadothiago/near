Every queer nightlife map has a gap where nobody's checked yet, and on Near's Baixada Santista coverage that gap has been Praia Grande. **Canto dos Ursos**, a karaoke room in Boqueirão that leans hard into bear-community branding — the name means "Bear's Corner," and the 🐻 shows up everywhere from the Instagram handle to the door — closes it.

## What the room actually is

This isn't a generic LGBTQ+ bar that happens to draw a bear crowd; it's built around that specific scene on purpose. The mic is the anchor — karaoke nights run under names like "Karaokê Top" — but the calendar rotates through a real spread of formats rather than repeating the same Friday: "Festa da Peruca" (wig night), a Madonna-themed set, "Flashback" throwback nights, and a running "Canto Viciante" residency. A recent one, "Festa Sem Filtro" (No-Filter Party) on September 1, ran three DJs back to back — João Dias, Edu Pacheco Júnior, and Detzel — for R$30 at the door with a R$15 minimum consumption, the kind of specific, checkable detail that tells you this is a working venue with an actual door policy, not an address someone half-remembers.

It runs Thursday through Sunday, and at 18.3K Instagram followers with posts landing multiple times a week, it's not coasting on old reviews the way plenty of "gay bar" search results for smaller Brazilian beach towns turn out to be — Near checked, and a couple of other Praia Grande leads from the same search pass had gone quiet since 2023. This one hasn't.

## Why Praia Grande, and why now

Near's operator lives in Baixada Santista, and the region's queer-nightlife coverage so far has clustered in Santos and São Vicente — <NearLink slug="quiosque-da-cris-sao-vicente">Quiosque da Cris</NearLink>'s beach kiosk, <NearLink slug="cabaret-latino-teatro-eskyna-santos">Cabaret Latino</NearLink> at Teatro Clube da Eskyna, <NearLink slug="amuse-beach-club-sao-vicente">Amuse Beach Club</NearLink>. Praia Grande, the next town down the coast and one of the region's biggest by population, had nothing. A bear-specific room with its own real rotation of themed nights is a stronger opener for that gap than a generic "also gay-friendly" bar would have been — it's a room with an actual identity, not a footnote.

## What Near couldn't confirm — and won't guess at

A general web search turned up a claim that the venue's hours had been adjusted while it awaited a city licensing decision. Near went looking for a real source for that — the venue's own channels, the city government's own site — and couldn't find one anywhere traceable. So it's not in this piece. Near would rather leave a detail out than publish something a search engine's own summary asserted without a link behind it. If that changes, or if you've been and the hours on the door don't match what's above, tell Near — that's exactly the kind of correction this page exists to take.
