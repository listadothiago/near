Grano Ricco opened in Centro in June 2025 — young enough that it hasn't accumulated the review volume of São Carlos's older bakeries yet, but with a consistency in what's there that's worth noting. It's part of a small regional chain with sister locations in Piracicaba, Rio Claro and Sorocaba, built around naturally-fermented viennoiserie and Italian-style breadmaking, and it shares its Centro space with Lattiera, an artisanal gelateria under the same roof and, per the owner's own Instagram posts, effectively the same team.

## The packaging is doing PR work the food doesn't have to

One reviewer specifically praised the packaging on a takeaway order of two croissants — one plain, one filled with almond cream — calling the presentation "impecável" and crediting the brand for clearly caring about the details. That's a small thing, but it's the kind of specific, unprompted praise that's harder to fake than a generic five-star rating. Another review calls out a scrambled-eggs-and-bacon breakfast plate as generously served and genuinely tasty, suggesting the menu goes past pastry into a real breakfast option.

![A jam-filled folhado pastry on a wooden board, from Grano Ricco's own Instagram, São Carlos](https://near.tips/places/grano-ricco-sao-carlos/hero.jpg)

Budget-wise, Google's own diner-reported range is R$40-60 per person, based on 55 people who logged what they spent — on the higher end for São Carlos, which tracks with the operator's own framing of this as the pricier pick on this list. Reviewers who mention cost tend to pair it with a "worth it" rather than a complaint, at least in the sample Google surfaces for this specific São Carlos location.

## A quick honesty note on sourcing

A wider web search turned up a sharply negative complaint about pricing and service quality attached to the Grano Ricco name — but nothing matching it shows up in this São Carlos location's own Google reviews, and a sister branch in Rio Claro carries a noticeably lower rating elsewhere online. Rather than risk pinning another location's bad night on this one, that complaint isn't repeated here.
