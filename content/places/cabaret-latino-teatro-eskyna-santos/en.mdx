"Cabaret Latino" is the next edition of a recurring drag cabaret night at <NearLink slug="teatro-clube-da-eskyna-santos">Teatro Clube da Eskyna</NearLink>, the small independent arts hub in Santos's Centro Histórico that stacks a black-box theater, a thrift shop, a record store, and a café-bar into one address. The venue announced the date on [its own Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/DchOD52Rhjh/) (@teatroclubedaeskyna) on August 26, 2026: Friday, September 11, at 9pm, at the venue's usual address on Rua Dom Pedro II.

This edition is headlined by drag performer Magentinha, described in the announcement as taking over the stage to run the night — "comandar o palco" — with a DJ set from Rafa Rouxinol, who's played the venue's parties before, including its pre-Carnival Baile La Mascarada earlier this year. The announcement frames it as "quente e beeeem latina" — hot and very Latin — leaning into a specific theme rather than repeating a generic drag-night format.

Ticket details, straight from the post: early tickets ("1º lote," first batch) run R$15 for a single entry or R$25 for a pair, with the clear implication that the price rises once that batch sells out — worth buying ahead rather than assuming door pricing will match. One detail worth flagging as a genuinely nice touch: anyone arriving in drag ("drag montada") doesn't pay to get in, a small but real signal about who this room is actually built for. The event is marked 18+.

One thing worth knowing before you make plans around it: everything above comes from the venue's own Instagram announcement. There's no separate ticketing site or press listing to cross-check it against — normal enough for a one-off night at a small independent space, where the poster goes up a fortnight ahead and that's the whole of the publicity. It does mean details can move without much warning. Check @teatroclubedaeskyna for any change to the date, the lineup, or which lote is currently selling before you head out, and if you're going in drag, you can skip the ticket question entirely.
