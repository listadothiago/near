Caverna da Gordura opened in Jardim Paraíso in September 2018 as a food-truck-style burger operation and never really stopped acting like one — the menu still reads like a truck's board that outgrew itself: loaded fries, a short beer list, burgers named after the thing they're stuffed with rather than a chef's-table conceit. It sits a few blocks from bus lines that run straight to USP's São Carlos campus, which explains a lot about who's actually in the room on a Tuesday.

## What people actually order

The [Porco na Lama](https://restaurantguru.com/Caverna-da-Gordura-Food-Truck-Sao-Carlos/menu) — pulled pork, house barbecue sauce, Catupiry — is the dish reviewers name without being asked, alongside the "Boi Bandido." Both show up repeatedly enough in Google's review summary that they're not a fluke pick. The fries menu is its own small economy: plain batata frita runs about R$27 for roughly 350g, the bacon-and-cheddar-sauce version about R$38, and a pulled-pork-and-Catupiry loaded version tops out near R$47, per the venue's own [iFood](https://www.ifood.com.br/delivery/sao-carlos-sp/caverna-da-gordura-burguer-jardim-paraiso/6ffb3a40-c9ab-4cec-8663-bfd139bd1432) and [Goomer](https://caverna-da-gordura.goomer.app/) delivery menus.

![Caverna da Gordura's storefront on Rua Capitão Adão Pereira de Souza Cabral in Jardim Paraíso, São Carlos](https://near.tips/places/caverna-da-gordura-sao-carlos/hero.jpg)

Budget-wise, this reads as genuinely mid-range for a sit-down burger bar rather than fast food: Google's own diner-reported range is R$40-60 per person, based on 85 people who logged what they spent — a wider and more honest number than any single menu item, since it folds in the beer.

## The room gets loud, and mostly that's the point

One Local Guide who's been coming for two years puts it plainly: the burger "não precisa acompanhar molho algum, é completo por si só" — doesn't need a sauce, it's complete on its own. That's the kind of confidence a menu earns after eight years of not changing much. Not every recent review is glowing — one diner this month flagged a delivery order that ran past an hour with the kitchen citing a shortage of delivery riders that night, a fair complaint about takeout logistics rather than the food itself, and worth knowing if you're ordering in rather than sitting down.

It opens at 6pm Monday through Saturday and tends to fill up fast once it does, so arriving right at open is the practical move if a table matters more than a bar stool.
