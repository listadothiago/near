I'm building this whole web app via MCP with Claude and OpenAI. It's 2026, so we're deep in a *slot-machine* era, with everything turning into gambling — gamblification. The slot-machine dynamic à la **Hooked** (editor, please don't link that Silicon Valley Necronomicon, which has the power to summon unspeakable horrors, thank you!) is blatant, and it's quicksand. It's also the face of [Enshittification](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9780374619329/enshittification/) (editor, you can link this one, we like Cory Doctorow).

My argument isn't complicated: ChatGPT or Claude ask you for a little cash (microtransactions) or a lot of cash (credit packs), you feed it all into the machine and hope for the best. Sometimes it's good, sometimes it's bad, sometimes it's terrible, and every once in a while maybe it's really good, perhaps one day great? So you feed in more credits. It's gambling. Slot-machine energy.

It's kind of shitty, honestly.
