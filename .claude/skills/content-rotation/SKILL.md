Maintain 60/30/10 output shares. Tier 1 rotates; Tier 2 uses a city burst; Tier 3
uses its one-post share. Preserve already scoped batch allocations even where
new tier membership differs. Select overdue work; no city disappears when held.

receive a documented chief-editor maintenance-only exception, never publication credit.

region too. Do not treat one borough as complete coverage of every NYC scope.

refreshes, collections and attempts do not count. Carry the saved batch's quota
shortfall explicitly; do not silently replace its remaining candidates. NITE-PORTER
and the required prep roles follow the hotel contract before selection.
