# Rotation reorder

Use at the start of a full round or when explicitly asked to reorder. Read
`docs/workflows/content-rotation.md`, existing run manifests and the current
`content-rotation/SKILL.md` and `content/post-plan.md` state. A skill-edit task
must not start a round or replace the live queue.

1. SEO attempts every saved URL in `content/trends-feeds.md`, adjusting time windows
   when useful, and compares relevant demand across all six locale markets.
   Record sources, time and volume basis. Weight by observed comparable volumes;
   normalized Trends scores are not absolute search volume. If volume data is
   unavailable, label weights unknown and avoid invented precision.
2. Trendsetter scans AAN/preferred independent sources and relevant-language
   local reporting/socials. Save concise supported patterns and blocked sources.
   Use this with SEO to choose each city's beats/lenses; CMO reviews coverage mix
   and the chief editor approves editorial choices. Keep role verdicts distinct.
3. Reconcile the union of existing tier lists and unresolved obligations. SEO may
   move/add cities, adjust tiers/shares and select the new starting point. Never
   remove a city or reset earned credit. Record before/after, rationale and an
   explicit lower-frequency Tier 4 schedule for new outside-tier wildcards.
4. Write the result to the new round's manifest and synchronize the rotation
   skill's tier/state sections and post-plan's pointer. Do not reorder an already
   scoped unfinished batch; carry its output into the round's coverage accounting.
   With blocked demand evidence, preserve the prior ordering and log the failed
   reorder attempt; a fetch failure is not permission to pretend a demand ranking.
5. Before handing to the coordinator, verify every destination, writer, filter,
   column and guide obligation is accounted for, five hotel slots are present,
   tier shares are coherent and remaining debt survives. Use the quiet-city
   exception only after research and maintenance, with chief-editor judgment.

Return the concise queue change, evidence limitations and next owed work. Record
reusable judgments in trendsetter/CMO brains; do not paste the whole feed dump into
all roles or reopen already-settled research.
