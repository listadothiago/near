# Multi-city SEO advice: fact check and Near actions

2026-09-07 — Codex (GPT-6), near-seo + AEO + Product Trio review.

The operator supplied a [LinkedIn compilation](https://www.linkedin.com/top-content/marketing/seo-techniques/strategies-for-managing-multi-city-seo-rankings/), including Noel Ceta’s architecture advice and Dan Hinckley’s city-level ranking observations. It contains useful ideas, but its absolute rules do not establish Google requirements. Near is an editorial guide, not a chain of service-business branches.

| Claim | Finding | Near decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Always nest location URLs; flat URLs are wrong | Overstated. Google recommends simple, intelligible URLs; it does not mandate a state/city/neighborhood path. | Keep existing indexed URLs. Build a useful hierarchy through links and location membership (P1.15), without a speculative URL migration. |
| Every URL needs state and city, must be lowercase and under 70 characters | No universal 70-character rule is established by Google’s guide. Hyphens are recommended; consistent case prevents duplicate URL variants. Geographic disambiguation is useful where needed. | Use readable, consistent slugs. Include geography to clarify identity, not to satisfy an invented limit. |
| Never target a city on an individual place page | Unsupported as an absolute. A place’s city is useful identifying context. | Hubs answer broad discovery needs; venue pages answer specific venue questions. Keep truthful city/address information. |
| Hubs prevent cannibalization | Hubs help navigation, but are no guarantee against competing URLs or poor rankings. | Audit query-to-page intent and internal links, then inspect actual Search Console data (P0.10/P1.20). |
| Every location needs LocalBusiness, Service, Review, coordinates and hours markup | Too broad. Structured data must describe the actual entity and visible, supported facts. A guide hub is not a business branch; a park is not necessarily a LocalBusiness. | Retain appropriate Place markup, review more specific types where applicable, and never fabricate reviews, services or opening hours (P1.18). |
| Unique local content and useful links matter | Supported directionally by Google’s people-first content and link guidance. Each page should have a useful purpose and reliable local facts. | Strengthen existing hubs and relevant venue links; avoid boilerplate city swaps and doorway pages. No arbitrary quota of links. |
| 15–20 pages or 100+ pages unlock organic traffic | These are planning guesses, not verified eligibility thresholds or traffic promises. | Measure impressions, indexed pages and useful coverage. Do not create pages to reach a supposed ranking threshold. |
| City-level ranking observations can differ from state-level observations | Plausible; the supplied post reports one query in a qualitative test. Its numerical results are not independently reproducible from the post. | Pilot a small, consistent city/query/device sample once the indexation audit is complete; do not extrapolate a percentage uplift to Near. Search Console country filters are not city-level rank measurements. |

Primary references reviewed:

- [Google URL structure guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/url-structure): readable URLs, hyphens, consistent case and excessive parameter combinations.
- [Google link guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable): crawlable links with descriptive anchor text and useful context.
- [Google people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content): original value and reliable sourcing.
- [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies): doorway and scaled-content abuse risks.
- [Google LocalBusiness structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business): applicable entities and properties, with no guarantee of rich-result display.

## Work extracted

1. **P0.10, Must:** establish the actual indexation baseline and causes before increasing page count for SEO.
2. **P1.15, Should:** regional/city/neighborhood membership and navigable hubs, preserving existing URLs.
3. **P1.20, Should:** audit hub-to-place and place-to-hub links, distinct page intent and geography-specific titles; choose one covered cluster for a measurable pilot.
4. **P1.18, Should:** assess structured data by page/entity type and compare it with visible facts.
5. **P1.20, Could:** controlled city-level SERP sampling with query, location, device, date and ranking URL recorded. No new paid tool or automatic campaign authorized.

Do not infer real venue staff biographies, customer reviews or Google Business Profile ownership for Near from a service-business marketing template. Cite actual reporting and the venue’s public listing where useful.
