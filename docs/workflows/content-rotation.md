# Rotation and hotel coverage contract

Operator feedback captured 2026-09-09; supersedes older one/three-hotel targets
and seed-only completion wording. Owners: SEO + trendsetter + CMO + chief editor.

## Units and completion

A **turn** is a bounded execution session. A **full rotation round** is the
persistent coverage cycle across every tier destination, active writer, filter,
column and the guides section. It can span many turns; ten posts or 30 articles
cannot silently stand in for the full roster. `near-batch-30` still caps a batch
at 30 new originals; its articles also credit the active round where applicable.

Maintain `content/_rotation-runs/<round-id>/manifest.json` when a round actually
starts. Record prior queue/snapshot path, tier membership and shares, owed
city/writer/filter/column/guide slots, due dates, hotel target/count, selected
beats, article slugs, publication evidence, exceptions and next owed work.
Preserve completed work and existing `content/post-plan.md` queues on resume.
Do not create an empty active round while merely editing this workflow.

A full round owes at least one new published piece to every tiered destination
(including due wildcards), active writing persona, filter, column and the guides
section, with higher tiers getting proportionally more output. A useful piece can
satisfy multiple real obligations; do not bolt irrelevant tags or bylines onto it.
Keep public collaborators credited only for real contributions. Planned/unlaunched
columns/personas are explicit dependencies, not silently marked covered. Track
column cadence throughout the round; do not delay a weekly issue until round end.

Every full rotation and every batch-30 must include **at least five new curated
hotels**, with normal six-locale gates. Five is the explicit minimum; the later
rhetorical reference to three does not lower it. Attempts, existing hotel refreshes
and a hotel collection do not count as new hotels. Failed gates leave a named
shortfall; no filler or pretend completion. Ordinary standalone refreshes retain
the at-least-one-hotel attempt unless part of a full round/batch.

## Reordering without losing destinations

Run `rotation-reorder` at the beginning of a new round. SEO may reorder, move or
add cities, create tiers and change shares using checked multilingual demand.
No city may be removed. Reconcile the union of BACKLOG section 2.2, the rotation
skill and preferred destinations; aliases must preserve coverage obligations,
including city/region distinctions. Log conflicts instead of silently discarding
cities from the longer list. Preserve spent counters and unfinished batch scope.

At every selection, pick overdue obligations within the recorded tier shares;
reordering cannot erase debt or restart at a favourite city. A newly found
wildcard outside regular tiers enters persistent **Tier 4**, lower-frequency than
Tier 3. Record its next due round and relative cadence explicitly. Every Tier 4
city gets coverage on its due round; lower frequency never means forgotten.
SEO sets this cadence from evidence, rather than this document inventing a number.

## Quiet-city exception

For a seeded city's turn, try other relevant angles (outdoors/day trips, science,
community activity, small-shop events), local socials, Reddit and overlooked
independent sources. If nothing earns publication, clean up/refresh existing
coverage and record checked sources, angles rejected and next review date.
The chief editor can accept `maintenance-only` for that city in that round.
This is an accounted exception, never a new-publication credit. An unseeded city
needs the source/candidate gate first; seed-only is progress, not full coverage.
Unresolved seed/quality/access blockers keep the round incomplete, with the next
eligible city allowed to proceed. Report fulfilled, excepted and blocked separately.

## Hotels as neighbourhood starting points

NITE-PORTER orchestrates and writes or genuinely collaborates with credit on every
hotel article. Choose the best writer for each hotel or Do Not Disturb issue
(e.g. FIT-BOT for fitness facilities); preserve weekly column ownership/continuity.
Before selection, SEO, trendsetter, affiliate-pr, backlink-pr, AEO and PARSER
prepare source-backed advice. Reuse their run packet for related pieces, retaining
per-hotel judgments. Invite the Product Trio at least once when tuning this
workflow; repeat when a material product/workflow decision changes.

Choose on editorial merit, then map nearby food, things to do and confirmed events
through verified, crawlable links. Reuse existing pins; commission missing useful
companions. Use the current flat geography model (neighbourhood/city/region/country
and broader context), without inventing schema fields or empty landing pages.
AEO checks standalone factual usefulness and clear relationships for human and
machine readers; PARSER checks reuse, provenance and freshness. Rates need check
date, stay dates, occupancy and fee/booking context, not a timeless 'from' price.

Near stays curated and fresh; it does not aim to list every hotel. Commercial
roles may research programs/prospects and draft pitches, but cannot bias coverage,
soften criticism, invent demand or send outreach without explicit authorization.
Approved affiliate links require clear disclosure. No existing program approval
is implied by this workflow. C-level assessment should use actual audience,
coverage and conversion evidence, not assume hotel coverage guarantees revenue.

When coverage becomes adequate, propose retiring the hotel quota and prioritizing
cleanup, then event refresh, then worthwhile new coverage and new destinations.
Record the decision; do not silently retire the quota or the operator's 1,000-place
meeting requirement. This maintenance direction applies across content types.
