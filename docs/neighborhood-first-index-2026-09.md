## The index — top 10

1. **Jimbōchō** (Tokyo) — historic book towns, retro charm, cafe culture.
2. **Borgerhout** (Antwerp) — community spirit, town hall square, multicultural.
3. **Barra Funda** (São Paulo) — industrial district turned creative hub;
   art studios, train tracks, nightlife. *Home market — prioritize.*
4. **Camberwell** (London) — independent spirit, food scene. *Tier 1.*
5. **Avondale** (Chicago) — bar and music culture.
6. **Mullae-dong** (Seoul) — old ironworks district, indie galleries,
   artisanal cafes, boutiques.
7. **Ménilmontant** (Paris) — steep streets, bohemian, views.
8. **Nakatsu** (Osaka) — post-war retro charm, hidden bars, record stores.
9. **Vallila** (Helsinki) — historic wooden houses, art spaces.
10. **Labone** (Accra) — food and nightlife pulse, creative studios.

## The index — expansion

- **Nguyen Thai Binh** (Ho Chi Minh City) — culinary + specialty coffee,
  French-colonial architecture against hyper-modern culture.
- **Anjos** (Lisbon) — diverse, activist-driven; community centers, vegan
  cafes, vintage clothing.
- **Digbeth** (Birmingham) — industrial-to-creative; street art,
  independent cinemas, warehouse food markets.
- **Red Hook** (Brooklyn, New York) — cobblestones, artist studios,
  waterfront dive bars, seafood shacks. *Tier 1.*
- **Perpetuo Socorro** (Medellín) — design, music production, open-air
  community hubs.
- **Burwood** (Sydney) — neon Chinatown night markets, regional Asian food.
- **Linden** (Johannesburg) — bookshops, artisan bakeries, cocktail bars.
- **Former French Concession** (Shanghai) — speakeasies, historic
  architecture, boutique multi-brand retail.
- **Quartieri Spagnoli** (Naples) — alleyways, street food, local character.
- **Bencoolen** (Singapore) — arts and design district, museums, galleries.
- **Endoume** (Marseille) — fishing-port charm, relaxed culinary scene.
- **Plateau-Mont-Royal** (Montréal) — outdoor staircases, parks, indie
  boutiques, poutine.
- **The Liberties** (Dublin) — whiskey distilleries, tech hubs, art-school
  energy.
- **North Melbourne** — Victorian storefronts, specialty coffee,
  community-led art.
- **Portales** (Mexico City) — street food markets, antique bazaars.
